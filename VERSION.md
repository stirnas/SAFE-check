## Known Limitations (Phase 2)

The following features are not yet implemented:
- Contradiction search (works well for well-supported claims)
- Ethical audit (6-principle system)
- Claim extraction automation (manual curation available)
- Full fundamental gate (simplified F adjustment in production)

These do not block production deployment and are scheduled for Q4 2026 / Q1 2027.
